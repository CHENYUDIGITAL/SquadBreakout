Arrow Tap Go! - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026 | Effective Date: July 30, 2026

1. Overview

Arrow Tap Go! (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects the privacy rights of all users (“you”, “your”) of our TikTok mini-game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use, store, protect, and disclose your information, and your rights regarding your personal data when you use the Arrow Tap Go! game service on the TikTok platform.

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Arrow Tap Go! mini-game and does not apply to third-party platforms, services, or websites that you may access through external links.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited information necessary to operate, maintain, and optimize the Arrow Tap Go! game experience. The collected information includes:

2.1 Automatically Collected Usage Data

When you launch and play Arrow Tap Go!, we automatically collect non-identifiable and platform-authorized usage data, including:

  • Game interaction data: gameplay duration, level progress, merge activity, in-game behavior, and feature usage statistics;
  • Device & technical data: device type, operating system, browser/ runtime version, anonymous device identifiers, and basic technical log data;
  • Performance data: game load status, error logs, crash reports, and service stability data.

2.2 Platform Authorized Data

We may receive limited, aggregated, non-personal user data provided by the TikTok platform in accordance with TikTok’s developer policies, solely for the purpose of operating and optimizing the mini-game service. We do not independently collect your TikTok password, private messages, phone number, or sensitive personal identity information.

2.3 Voluntarily Provided Information

If you contact our support team for feedback, bug reports, or assistance, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your feedback content and contact information you choose to submit.

3. How We Use Your Information

We only use collected data for legitimate service purposes, including:

  • Providing, maintaining, and optimizing the Arrow Tap Go! gameplay experience;
  • Fixing bugs, resolving technical errors, and improving game stability and performance;
  • Analyzing user gameplay trends to optimize game mechanics and user experience;
  • Responding to user support requests and feedback;
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements and platform policies.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal or usage data to any third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your personal data with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Required by law, court order, or governmental regulatory requirements;
  • Necessary to protect our legal rights, property, or safety, as well as the rights and safety of users or the public;
  • With your explicit voluntary consent for specific data sharing.

We may share anonymous, aggregated, non-personal statistical data for service optimization and operational analysis, which cannot be used to identify any individual user.

5. Data Security

We adopt industry-standard technical and operational security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, loss, or misuse. These safeguards include data encryption, secure server deployment, regular system audits, and access permission control mechanisms.

While we implement strict security protocols to protect your data, no internet-based service or data storage system can guarantee absolute security. We continuously optimize our security measures to reduce data security risks.

6. Data Retention

We retain your gameplay data and technical log data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which the data was collected, including service operation, fault troubleshooting, and experience optimization. Anonymous statistical data may be retained indefinitely for long-term service improvement.

Once the data is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymize your data in accordance with applicable laws and platform rules.

7. User Rights

Depending on applicable local data protection laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your relevant data, as well as the right to data portability and withdrawal of consent.

If you wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us via the official Arrow Tap Go! support channel on TikTok. We will respond to your valid request within the time limit required by law.

8. Children’s Privacy

We attach great importance to minor users’ privacy protection. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and find that your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete the relevant data.

9. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically to adapt to service adjustments, technical upgrades, and legal requirement changes. We will post the updated policy on the relevant game page or TikTok platform entry with a revised update date. Your continued use of Arrow Tap Go! after the policy update constitutes your acceptance of the new terms.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or disputes regarding this Privacy Policy or our data processing practices, please contact us through the official support channel of Arrow Tap Go! on TikTok.
